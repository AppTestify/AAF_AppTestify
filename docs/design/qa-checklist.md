# Design QA Checklist (v1)

## Cross-Route Visual Checks
- Verify spacing rhythm and card elevation consistency on workspace routes.
- Validate heading hierarchy and section-intro readability on dark backgrounds.
- Confirm status-chip colors meet contrast in both light and dark themes.

## Interaction Checks
- Keyboard focus visibility for buttons, links, form inputs, and table rows.
- Loading, empty, and error states present for every data table.
- Button disabled/loading states prevent duplicate actions.

## Accessibility Checks
- No low-contrast text in dark subpage bands.
- Form labels present for all fields.
- Critical actions are reachable and usable via keyboard only.
